Why I Like Futurama

Futurama is a cartoon made by Matt Groening, the same guy who made The Simpsons. It started on Fox in 1999. It is about Fry, a pizza delivery guy in New York who accidentally gets frozen on New Year's Eve 1999 and wakes up in the year 3000.

He ends up working at Planet Express, a delivery company run by his very distant nephew, Professor Farnsworth. The crew delivers stuff all over space and something usually goes wrong.

My favourite character is Bender, the robot who steals everything and drinks too much. I like this show because it is really funny, but it also sneaks in science and math jokes with adult humor. A few episodes are also pretty sad, which surprised me for a cartoon.
